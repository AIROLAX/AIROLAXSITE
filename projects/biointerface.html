Biointerface

AI + Interactive Art

Description

Biointerface explores the intersection of artificial intelligence and biological data visualization. This interactive installation uses real-time biometric data to generate dynamic visual patterns, creating a bridge between human physiology and digital art.

The system captures heart rate, brainwave patterns, and other physiological signals to create evolving visual landscapes that respond to the user's emotional and physical state.

Technologies

Machine Learning Biometric Sensors Real-time Processing Interactive Visualization Python OpenCV

Concept

The concept explores how technology can serve as a mirror to our internal biological processes, making the invisible visible through art. By translating physiological data into aesthetic experiences, Biointerface creates a new form of human-computer interaction that is both intimate and universal.
